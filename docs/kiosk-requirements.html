Entry & Session

5 requirements
As someone approaching, I see the welcome screen already displayed (the kiosk's unengaged mode) — never dark — so I know what the kiosk is and how to use it before I touch it.
Launch
As a user, when I touch the screen it starts a clean session, so I do not inherit the previous person's session.
Launch
As a user, I can end my session deliberately, so anything I typed (like a phone number) is cleared from the screen.
Launch
As a Spanish-speaking user, I can read the kiosk in Spanish.
Roadmap
As a first-time user, I get a short cue about what each section is for and how to use the kiosk.
Roadmap

Requests & Handoff

18 requirements
As a user with a phone, I scan a QR code and take the resource with me.
Launch
As a user with a phone, I can text the directions to myself.
Launch
As a user, before I share any details with CSAH, I see a short consent note explaining what's collected and that it's used only for CSAH to follow up with me.
Launch
As CSAH, the kiosk stores nothing itself — a number used to send a resource to a phone is purged on send, and any details sent to CSAH go to the CSAH database, not the kiosk.
Launch
As a user who needs a ride, I can get the transport number without placing a call from the kiosk.
Launch
As a user, I understand what a caseworker is and what they can do for me.
Launch
As a user, I can see where to walk in and speak to someone in person.
Launch
As a user without a phone, during business hours I can ask CSAH to send an outreach worker to the kiosk, share optional details that help them find me, and I'm told to stay nearby.
Launch
As CSAH, I know exactly what each kiosk request records — request type, kiosk, location, and time, plus whatever the user chooses to share (name optional; a phone number, or details to help find them) — held in the CSAH database.
Launch
As a user who submitted a request, I get a clear message about what happens next: a CSAH callback if I gave a phone number; an outreach worker coming to this location as soon as one is available (stay nearby) if I have no phone; or CSAH's hours and to return during business hours if they're closed — always with 211 and the 24/7 emergency options.
Launch
As a user asking for help outside outreach hours, I am routed (or offered) somewhere that will answer.
Roadmap
As a user, I take info with me by entering my number on an on-screen dialpad or scanning a QR code — no keyboard needed for take-away.
Launch
As a user, I can send a request to CSAH from the kiosk — for a caseworker or a general inquiry — and it reaches the outreach team, whether or not I have my own phone.
Launch
As a user, I only see "request sent" once CSAH has actually received it — then I get the what-happens-next message; if it fails, I get a number to call plus Get Help Now.
Launch
As CSAH, kiosk requests can't be spammed or duplicated.
Launch
As a user, I can request emergency transportation from CSAH under Get Help Now — handled right away, any hour, separate from the callback queue.
Launch
As a user, I can fill out a short request form — pick a request type (caseworker or general inquiry), optionally add my name, and give either a phone number for a callback or details that help outreach find me — after a consent note, before I submit.
Launch
As a user, I can type into the request form using an on-screen keyboard that appears only in this form — browsing and search stay keyboard-free — placed within reach and meeting accessibility standards.
Launch

Crisis / Get Help

2 requirements
As a user in crisis, I can get emergency help from any screen, at any hour, without my own phone — the crisis numbers (211, 988, 911, SPD Hope Unit, CSAH) to call from my own phone, CSAH textable from the kiosk, and emergency transport.
Launch
As a user asking for help outside outreach hours, the kiosk shows me the 24/7 options that will answer — 911, 988, SPD Hope Unit, 211, and the CSAH line — with honest off-hours messaging.
Launch

2 requirements
As CSAH, the kiosk cannot be used to reach the OS or a web browser.
Launch
As a user, if the kiosk loses its connection, I'm told the info may be out of date and the actions that need a connection are clearly disabled — not dead taps.
Launch

Handled in the Companion App, not the kiosk

"As a CSAH outreach worker, I receive a request sent from a kiosk, claim it, and record its outcome in Mission Control with a status and notes."

"As CSAH, an inbound kiosk request reaches a monitored queue — a claim in the Companion App plus a backup SMS to the on-call lead; the alert says only 'new request,' with the details held in the queue, not the notification."
