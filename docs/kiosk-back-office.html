Back Office Flow

How CSAH runs the kiosk once it's live — in Mission Control, one dashboard, one shared dataset, pushed to every unit.

All management of this area lives inside the Companion App. It's documented here since there is overlap between the Kiosk and the App.

Mission Control

The shared content dashboard CSAH uses to run the kiosk and app. It has tabs for the kiosk, the app, and future PIT, plus a separate Reporting tab which houses both Kiosk useage data & App operations data. The launch version is deliberately simple; its roadmap is broader operations (including provider-submitted content with CSAH approval)

Run It — Ongoing, CSAH in Mission Control

Edit content, page by page

Edit resources and copy on a simple page-based screen; saving updates the units automatically.

Publish alerts

Publish a weather or emergency alert — title, message, effective time, recommended actions, shelter/service locations and hours, transport info, emergency numbers. It reaches every live unit near-instantly and shows on the Home Screen until it expires or you remove it.

Manage & maintain resources

Add, edit, or retire a resource and its hours, eligibility, phone, address, and tags — and keep it current: a named owner and cadence, with "hours may change — call ahead" as the safeguard.

Set crisis routing & copy

The Get Help Now numbers (211 / 988 / 911 / Hope Unit / CSAH) and the honest after-hours message shown when the outreach team is off.

Resolve the morning queue

Requests made after hours are held in a queue; the outreach team resolves them first thing each morning, setting a status and notes on each.

Reporting tab

A light view for funders and admin — CSV export, filtered by date, location, or service category. It surfaces the measures we track (see Objectives & Experience → How we'll know it's working).

How It Connects to the App

A kiosk request becomes a claim

When someone requests a caseworker or a general inquiry at a kiosk, it lands in the Companion App's claim queue — routed to the outreach team's shift lead or outreach director, with a backup text to a monitored inbox. Staff work the queue, setting a status and notes on each. The request carries the type, kiosk, location, and time, plus the name/phone or identifying details the person chose to share — held in the queue, not in the alert. One open request per unit until resolved; after hours it holds for the team to resolve first thing next morning. (Emergency transport is handled immediately under Get Help Now. Defined in the Companion App work; shown here as the boundary.)

Data governance. Role-based access; all content and session data lives on CSAH-controlled infrastructure; device monitoring is limited to unit health — no user-activity data leaves CSAH. Request contact details (name/phone or identifying info) are stored on CSAH-controlled infrastructure with role-based access and a retention policy aligned to CSAH records retention and HUD / State of Georgia / local requirements; the kiosk itself stores nothing.
