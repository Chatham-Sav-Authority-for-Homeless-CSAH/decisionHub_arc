Project Overview
What We're Building

The Compass Platform is a purpose-built coordination and data system for CSAH's outreach team. Today, coordination happens over personal phones, paper handouts, and disconnected systems. This platform replaces that friction with real-time, case-linked communication — so every member of CSAH's team is working from the same information, at the same time. Built as a foundation that can expand to partner agencies in future phases.

The platform has two surfaces: a staff app used by CSAH's outreach team for case tracking, push coordination, and resource reference in the field; and a network of public kiosks at high-traffic community sites where individuals can find resources and text them directly to their own phone. Both connect to a single data layer — any update is reflected everywhere, instantly.

Every interaction — who made contact, what was offered, what happened — is logged against a case record. This builds the dataset CSAH needs to demonstrate compassionate diversion, track housing outcomes, and tell the true story of how this community responds to homelessness.

What the Platform Does
🖥️

Public Kiosks

Touchscreen stations at key community sites give individuals direct access to the full resource directory. From the kiosk, anyone can text resources directly to their own phone — no app, no account needed. Kiosk-initiated requests can also trigger real-time alerts to CSAH's outreach team.

Kiosk
📍

Resource Directory

An always current directory of shelter, meals, healthcare, transportation, and services — accessible to field workers in the app and to individuals at public kiosks. No more paper handouts — one source of truth, maintained once, accessible everywhere.

Kiosk Core App Feature
🔔

Crisis Coordination

Push notifications keep CSAH's outreach team coordinated in real time — case handoffs, crisis response, and field coverage. Every alert is geotagged and logged against a case record, building a verified record of compassionate outreach ahead of any enforcement action.

Core App Feature
📋

Case Tracking - Key Data

Each individual is a case. Interactions, referrals, and outcomes are logged by CSAH's outreach team — building the dataset needed to demonstrate compassionate diversion. A truth-telling tool for the data that matters.
