Kiosk Hardware Monitoring $10 – $288 MetroClick's confirmed formal quote (Jul 23, 2026) combines both of their recurring fee line items: Yearly Data Fee ($450/unit/yr) + Yearly SaaS fee ($702/unit/yr net of the 10% nonprofit discount) = $1,152/unit/yr ($96/unit/mo) — $288/mo for 3 kiosks. Low end ($10/mo) reflects the theoretical lean hardware-only + ARC's-own-MDM path (Scalefusion-style, ~$2–4/device/mo), not yet re-quoted.
Cellular Connectivity $9 – $60 General market estimate for Managed IoT SIM (multi-carrier, e.g. Hologram-style) — the recommended lean approach per the Kiosk Connectivity comparison doc, still marked VERIFY there. This is the raw carrier/data cost, independent of kiosk hardware vendor — MetroClick (or any kiosk vendor) doesn't have real market insight into telecom pricing, so their bundled quote isn't used here. See Kiosk Monitoring above for MetroClick's actual recurring charges.
SMS (Twilio) $10 – $20 Estimated at 400 texts/month (~13/day total, ~4–5/day per kiosk across 3 kiosks) — mainly the kiosk's text-a-resource feature, which the earlier 250/month figure didn't account for (that estimate only covered partner-app push-notification fallback). Worked estimate against Twilio's public rate card, assuming resource-info texts average 2 SMS segments each (shelter name/address/hours easily exceeds the 160-character single-segment limit): 800 segments × ~$0.0079 (~$6.32) + A2P 10DLC per-segment carrier surcharge (~$2.40) + phone number rental (~$1.15) + A2P 10DLC monthly campaign fee (~$1.50–$10, varies by tier) ≈ $11–$20/mo. This is a worked estimate from public pricing, not a live Twilio quote — verify current rates before treating as final. Provider leaning Twilio; not yet contracted.
Database (Supabase) $25 flat Budgeting Supabase Pro flat rather than starting on the free tier. Free tier has no backups — not an acceptable risk, even though row/bandwidth limits would likely allow staying on Free at launch scale otherwise.
Hosting $0 – $19 Netlify free tier covers MVP traffic comfortably (see Hosting comparison doc). $19/mo ceiling reflects Netlify Pro — priority support, 1TB bandwidth, 400 build minutes — as an upgrade path, not a default cost.
Domain Name $1 – $2 Standard annual domain registration (~$12–20/yr for a .org), prorated monthly.
Map / Tiles $0 – $10 Covers embedding a map directly in the app for wayfinding/directions instead of linking out. Easy to assume this is free the way consumer Google Maps is — it's technically a metered developer API, not a truly free product. That said, per ADR-008, cost is effectively a non-factor at CSAH's projected volume, especially combined with the lazy-caching approach for directions (fetch once, reuse for 30 days) — the small ceiling here is a buffer, not an expected charge. Self-hosted OpenStreetMap (OSM) tiles remain on the table as a $0 path regardless of which vendor is chosen. Vendor/approach not finalized — see ADR-008.
Possible additional costs — not included in the total above
  • ARC ongoing maintenance: Not a hosting/software line — this would be a separate service retainer between CSAH and ARC Consulting for post-launch feature work and support. No rate has been discussed; excluded from this doc entirely.
