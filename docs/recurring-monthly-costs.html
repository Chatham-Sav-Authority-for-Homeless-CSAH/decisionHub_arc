Cellular Connectivity $90 – $150 MetroClick's formal quote (Jul 23, 2026) confirms a Yearly Data Fee of $450/unit/yr ($37.50/mo) via a managed multi-carrier IoT SIM — $112.50/mo for 3 kiosks. Range kept slightly wider to account for other vendors: Meridian's formal quote (Jul 22) prices cellular as a one-time $452/unit hardware add-on with no recurring data cost itemized — confirm whether a separate carrier data plan is needed if Meridian is selected instead.
RDM / Kiosk Software $10 – $60 Reflects the lean, hardware-only-plus-ARC's-own-MDM path: Scalefusion (~$2–4/device/mo) up to SiteKiosk Online (~$10–20/device/mo) × 3 kiosks. Not finalized, and now a bigger swing than this range shows: MetroClick's formal quote (Jul 23) prices their own SaaS platform at $702/yr/unit net (~$175.50/mo for 3 units), and Meridian's formal quote (Jul 22) prices mZero Manage + Create SDK at $3,120/yr/unit (~$780/mo for 3 units, from year 2) — both confirmed, and both well above this range if the selected vendor's bundled software is kept rather than switching to hardware-only + ARC's own MDM. Whichever kiosk hardware vendor is selected will likely decide this line.
SMS (Twilio) $20 – $50 Estimated at ~250 outbound/inbound messages per month for case-linked messaging. Provider leaning Twilio; not yet contracted.
Hosting $0 – $25 Candidate platforms (Replit, Railway, Render, Vercel, Netlify) all offer free/hobby tiers likely sufficient at MVP traffic; budgeting to a paid tier as a safe planning ceiling. Platform not finalized.
Domain Name $1 – $2 Standard annual domain registration (~$12–20/yr for a .org), prorated monthly.
Possible additional costs — not included in the total above
  • Database (Supabase): Free tier likely covers MVP scale; Pro tier (if row/bandwidth limits are exceeded) runs roughly $25/mo. Confirm free-tier limits before committing either way.
  • ARC ongoing maintenance: Not a hosting/software line — this would be a separate service retainer between CSAH and ARC Consulting for post-launch feature work and support. No rate has been discussed; excluded from this doc entirely.
