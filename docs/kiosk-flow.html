Experience Flow

The path a person takes at the kiosk, end to end — plus the moments that branch off it.

On Every Screen

Always present, at any hour
Get Help Now — crisis help from any screen
Accessibility — contrast · large text · light/dark · volume
Audio — any keypad press or the audio-assist button starts the screen reader; operate by keypad + audio
Weather — conditions now; CSAH-published critical alerts take over
Back · Home · Done — Done clears the screen

The Main Path

Welcome → done
1

Welcome — unengaged mode

The kiosk always shows the welcome screen — its unengaged mode (the screensaver). It is never dark. It shows what this is, how to use it, live weather, and a "you are here" map, and doubles as the branding surface. A vision-impaired user can start audio from here with any keypad press or the audio-assist button.

↓
2

Touch to start

A touch switches the kiosk to engaged mode and offers a "Get Started" button through to the Home Screen. Each session starts clean — no information left from the person before.

↓
3

Home Screen

One simple grid of needs, icon-first with a clear text label on each tile, plus the greeting and live weather and time. No typing. Redesigned for the 55-inch display — more tiles, easier navigation.

  • Food + Showers
  • Shelters
  • Day Centers
  • Medical + Mental Health
  • Rent + Utilities
  • Veterans Services
  • Free Transportation
  • Pets
  • Public Camping
  • Find a Caseworker
  • ID Cards
  • Get Help Now
  • CSAH

Built for the 55-inch display in CSAH's brand (blue & yellow, lighthouse/compass), with the CSAH logo. The interactive zone sits within ADA reach.

↓
4

Browse a category

Tap a tile to see the closest options first. Filter by tapping chips, with a "clear all" reset — no search, no keyboard. Distance shown; eligibility up front.

↓
5

Resource page

One place shows everything it offers — photo, who it's for, hours, address, phone, distance.

↓
6

Act — a resource shows only what it supports

Go here

A place

Map directions from "you are here" (bus by default for distant sites), plus send the address to your phone.

Call

A phone line

The number, big, to dial from your own phone — or send it to yourself.

Reach CSAH

A request

Send a request to CSAH — a caseworker or a general question. Give a phone for a callback, or details so outreach can find you. Works with or without a phone.

ID Cards goes only to CSAH: how to apply for or replace an ID and what you need, then directions, text, or call to CSAH — no resource list.
↓
7

Take it with you

Send it to your phone with a QR code or a keypad — nothing stored. Everything also stays readable on-screen for anyone without a phone. No printer.

↓
8

Done

Tap Done, or walk away — after a short timeout (with a "still there?" warning first) the screen clears and returns to the welcome (unengaged) screen.

Designed against: we designed against the failure modes that make a kiosk useless — a no-phone dead-end at take-away; showing a resource someone isn't eligible for; showing a closed resource as open; a browse or filter that finds nothing with nowhere to go; and any interactive element placed out of ADA reach on the tall unit.

Moments That Branch Off the Main Path

The side flows

Get Help Now — crisis & urgent

Any screen→Crisis options→Call / text · or request a ride

Always one tap away. The kiosk shows the numbers to call from your own phone — it can't place calls itself: 211, 988, 911, the SPD Hope Unit, and CSAH. CSAH can also be texted from the kiosk, and emergency transportation lives here. Off-hours, it shows the lines that are staffed 24/7.

Find a Caseworker

Request→Confirm it's received→Outreach team (via the app)

You choose a callback (give a phone number) or, with no phone, share details that help an outreach worker find you at the kiosk — after a short consent note. "Sent" shows only once CSAH actually receives it; if it can't be confirmed, the kiosk shows a number to call plus Get Help Now. After hours, the request holds for the team to resolve first thing next morning. 211 and the 24/7 options show on every request screen.

Audio assistance (vision-impaired)

Press any key / audio-assist button→Screen reader starts + points to the jack→Move by keypad, listening

Any keypad press, or the on-screen audio-assist button, starts the ARC-built screen reader — no always-on voice, nothing announced aloud. From there you operate the entire kiosk by keypad and audio, plug in headphones for privacy, and can complete a request the same way.

Weather / emergency alert

CSAH publishes→Takes over welcome screen→Banner on Home Screen in use

A CSAH-published alert propagates to all live units near-instantly (via Mission Control), takes over the welcome screen at wake, and shows as a dismissible banner while in use. Automatically generated weather-alerts are on the roadmap.

See it on the map

A resource · Free Transport · "where am I"→Map: services + CAT routes + pins→Directions · send to phone

One shared map view shows every service, the CAT bus routes, and resource-category listings with pins. Viewable on-screen and sent to your phone.
