Verdict
Summary Strong accessibility (JAWS) and proven stability. One-time license is appealing for a grant budget. Weakest on shared data source and cloud-native on-demand updates — not designed for API-driven multi-surface sync. Best overall fit for this project. Nonprofit pricing, strong API and built-in CMS, 30-day trial to test before Dec 2026. Closest to project needs with least disruption to the existing SCAD-designed system. Best single-data-source architecture for kiosk + partner app. Strongest if the app eventually manages device-level data too. Weakest on accessibility — important for serving the homeless population. Closest philosophy to KioWare. Requires two products (SureLock + SureMDM) to match feature parity. Best if pure browser lockdown is the only priority and API/data-source needs are handled elsewhere.

Scores out of 10 reflect fit to Compass Project requirements only. Pricing is approximate as of 2025–2026 — verify with vendors and negotiate nonprofit/volume rates. KioWare's lower single-data-source score reflects that it was not originally designed for API-driven multi-surface data sharing; it remains a strong lockdown-first tool.
